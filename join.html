Join the AID Lab — open positions for Ph.D. students and postdocs

→ Recruiting · Fall 2027

We are looking for Ph.D. students and postdocs with a background in physics, applied math, complex systems, or machine learning. As an interdisciplinary group, we highly value curiosity, independence, and a willingness to read broadly and learn from others not in your immediate field.

Ph.D. students

Apply to UR Physics by December 15.

Apply to the University of Rochester Department of Physics & Astronomy Ph.D. program through the online application — the deadline is December 15. Mention Yuanzhao in your statement of purpose, and feel free to email him first with a paragraph about what you're curious about.

Postdocs

Open to applications now.

Yuanzhao welcomes postdoc inquiries on a rolling basis. Email him a short note on what you'd like to work on, along with a CV. If you're applying for your own fellowship, he's glad to host and help shape the proposal.

UR undergraduates

Independent study and summer research.

Enrolled UR undergrads can join for independent-study credit during the year. For the summer, the university funds research through the Schwartz Discover Grant and the department's research opportunities.
