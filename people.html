Principal investigator

Yuanzhao Zhang

Simons Empire Assistant Professor

Broadly, I am interested in systems that exhibit rich emergent behaviors while remaining amenable to theoretical analysis. Half jokingly, I like to say that I am into the simplest complex systems and not-too-nonlinear dynamics. Before joining the University of Rochester, I was an Omidyar Fellow at the Santa Fe Institute and a Schmidt Science Fellow at Cornell working with Steven Strogatz. I received my Ph.D. in Physics from Northwestern, advised by Adilson Motter.
